03 / Guide

How to take part

Use the form on your phone or computer. No account is needed.

Open the form

01

Choose a location

Listen for two minutes at Clementi 448. Open the form and tap the map where you listened.

02

Describe the sound

Add a short title and description. Enter the observation’s start date and time in Singapore time (UTC+8).

03

Rate the disturbance

Choose the main sound source and your activity. Rate the disturbance from 1 (not at all) to 5 (extremely).

04

Submit the report

Choose the record type. For a sample, select Hypothetical demo; for a form test, select Interface test. A photo is optional (JPEG or PNG, up to 2 MB). Avoid identifiable faces. Tick the public-sharing box and select Submit.

05

Check the confirmation

The success message confirms receipt. Reviewed reports can be read in the published report list.
