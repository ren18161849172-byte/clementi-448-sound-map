How to read the reports

Disturbance is rated from 1 (not at all) to 5 (extremely). It describes personal experience, not measured sound levels. Reports need review before publication.

The examples demonstrate the survey and map. A real study would need observations at different times and from different participant groups before drawing conclusions.
